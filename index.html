Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

1. Device IDs

Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA) and app-scoped anonymous IDs are not collected, stored, or transmitted. The app works fully offline.

2. Runtime Permissions

The app does not request runtime permissions. It does not access photos/media/files, camera, notifications, network, clipboard, location, or nearby devices.

3. Data Categories & Purpose

  • Usage data: local counters for “Done/Skip”, last action time, preferences (theme, show stats). Purpose: app functionality only.
  • No diagnostics, no advertising interaction data, no personal data.

4. Processing & Association

  • Processing location: on-device only. No upload or sync.
  • No linkage to personal identity. No cross-app tracking.

5. Third-Party SDKs

No third-party SDKs (no ads, no analytics, no attribution).

6. Consent & Controls

  • GDPR/UMP, iOS ATT: not applicable because no tracking/ads.
  • Data reset: Settings → Reset local data clears all local counters/preferences.

7. Sharing, Retention, Security

  • Sharing: none.
  • Retention: stored locally until the user resets or uninstalls the app.
  • Security: OS sandbox, no network transmission, minimal data.

8. Regional Rights

GDPR/CCPA/CPRA rights (access/delete/restrict) are satisfied by local-only storage and the in-app reset control. No data is sent to servers.

9. Contact

Email: see app store listing for the contact address.

10. Core Statement

The app is offline-first and processes data locally by default. If future cloud features are added, we will disclose details and obtain consent before any upload.
